## 1. Ground Axioms of the Field
The algebraic architecture of the Non-Archimedean Cosmological Model is mapped entirely within Conway’s Nimber Field $(\mathbf{On}_2)$. Elements of this field are ordinals (both finite and transfinite limit scales). 

The field is governed by two internal binary operations: **Nim-Addition ($\oplus$)** and **Nim-Multiplication ($\otimes$)**.

### Axiom I: Characteristic 2 Fields
Every element $x \in \mathbf{On}_2$ acts as its own unique additive inverse. The operational definition of subtraction ($\ominus$) collapses identically into addition:
$$x \oplus x = 0 \implies x \ominus y = x \oplus y$$

### Axiom II: Bitwise Discretization
For any two finite integers or ordinals expressed via their unique binary representations, addition is strictly non-carrying and map-equivalent to a bitwise Exclusive-OR (XOR) logic gate:
$$x \oplus y = \sum_{i=0}^{\infty} (x_i \,\,\Delta\,\, y_i) 2^i, \quad \text{where } \Delta \text{ is the XOR operator}$$

## 2. Transfinite Limit Behavior ($\omega$)
The model introduces the first limit ordinal, $\omega$, representing the absolute baseline boundary of infinity. In binary field columns, $\omega$ occupies a position strictly to the left of all finite positions ($2^{\infty}$).

### Theorem of Non-Dilution
*Let $x$ be a transfinite element such that $x \ge \omega$, and let $e$ be a localized finite variation such that $e < \omega$. The field coupling evaluates as an absolute additive stack:*
$$x \oplus e = x + e$$

### Proof of Algebraic Isolation
Because $x$ and $e$ occupy mutually exclusive binary columns, they share no overlapping internal bit rows:
$$(x \oplus e) \oplus e = x \oplus (e \oplus e) = x \oplus 0 = x$$
This proves that finite thermodynamic energy shifts or localized particle fluctuations ($\vec{\mathcal{E}}$) can never dissolve, diffuse, or dilute the transfinite background structural foundation ($\omega$).
